## 1) Content Brief
- **Audience:** Restaurants, cafés, and food trucks with an existing or planned site; consideration stage.
- **Pain:** The site looks fine, but visitors cannot tell what to do next.
- **So-what:** On a phone, visitors may meet the brand through the first few screens. Review those screens for the point, proof, and action.
- **Objective:** Qualified demand. Primary metric: link clicks → qualified DMs/Sofrito Session inquiries.
- **CTA (one):** Book Your Sofrito Session — https://sofritostudio.com/#book-now

## 2) Core idea
On a phone, a food-business website needs a clear point, proof, and action in the first few screens. Explain what those screens should communicate so the owner can self-diagnose, then offer a focused review.

## 3) Platform-ready copy

**Carousel headline (cover):**
> Make the first screens of your website answer the next question.

**Frames (one idea per frame, 5):**

- **Frame 2 — Screen 1: The point.**
  "The first screen should answer: who you are, what you make, and where you are. Make those answers easy to find before asking for a click."

- **Frame 3 — Screen 2: The proof.**
  "Show the plate, the people, or the place when it helps explain the offer. A generic hero image may not tell visitors what makes the business specific."

- **Frame 4 — Screen 3: The action.**
  "Use a screen for the action: order, call, directions, booking, or catering. Make that action easy to reach. The site should support a clear next step, not just describe the business."

- **Frame 5 — What most food sites actually show:**
  "A common pattern is a long introduction, a general image, and a contact form far below the first useful content. Review the order and move the useful information up."

- **Close:**
  "You may not need a full rebuild to improve the first screens. The Sofrito Session lasts 45 minutes. We review your site and identify practical next steps."
