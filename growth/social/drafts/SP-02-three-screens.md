## 1) Content Brief
- **Audience:** Restaurants/cafés/trucks with an existing (or planned) site; consideration stage.
- **Pain:** "My site looks fine — but it doesn't pull people in or take the order."
- **So-what:** Owners pay for a site to be seen, but the screens buyers actually judge on a phone are the first three — and most sites show the wrong ones.
- **Objective:** Qualified demand. Primary metric: link clicks → qualified DMs/session inquiries.
- **CTA (one):** Book the 90-minute web teardown session.

## 2) Core idea
On a phone, food-business owners get three seconds and three screens. Explain what those three screens must be, in order, so the owner can self-diagnose — then offer the Session that audits it for them.

## 3) Platform-ready copy

**Carousel headline (cover):**
> Your website has three seconds on a phone. It's showing the wrong screens.

**Frames (one idea per frame, 5):**

- **Frame 2 — Screen 1: The point.**
  "Before appetite, before photos — the first screen must answer: who are you, what do you make, where are you. If a buyer can't answer those in the time it takes to blink, they're gone."

- **Frame 3 — Screen 2: The proof.**
  "Screen two is where you show, not tell: the plate, the people, the place. This is the screen that does your selling — and the one most owners get wrong by defaulting to a generic hero image."

- **Frame 4 — Screen 3: The action.**
  "The third screen decides everything — order, call, directions, book, cater. If the action isn't two taps away, the site is a brochure with a lock on the door."

- **Frame 5 — What most food sites actually show:**
  "An intro paragraph nobody reads … a stock photo of a general vibe … and a contact form at the bottom of a scroll nobody finishes. Same three screens, wrong content."

- **Close:**
  "You don't need a full rebuild to fix the first three screens. A 90-minute Sofrito Session walks your actual site top-to-bottom and tells you the three fixes with the biggest effect."

**CTA line:**
> Book the teardown session → sofritostudio.com/session
