How the trap works

The site lives inside their platform

POS-bundled websites aren't standalone sites — they're features of the provider's system. Your domain might point there, but the pages, the design, and the content management all belong to the platform.

Switching POS means starting over

Outgrow your provider, find better rates, or just want a change? Your website doesn't come with you. You're rebuilding from zero — during the busiest transition your business will face.

You're stuck in their template

POS website builders offer a handful of layouts. Every restaurant on the platform ends up looking related — which is the opposite of what an independent restaurant needs.

One vendor controls two critical systems

When your payments and your public presence run through the same company, every pricing change, outage, or dispute puts both at risk at once.

The independent alternative

Separation is the whole strategy. Your POS handles payments. Your website handles your public presence. Neither can hold the other hostage — switch providers whenever the numbers say so, and your site doesn't blink.
