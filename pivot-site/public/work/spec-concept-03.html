From family-cook word-of-mouth to a real food brand

An unsolicited concept project: a beloved family kitchen with loyal neighbors but no name, no website, and no plan. This demonstrates how the studio could take the same cooking, give it identity and a launch-ready content set, and turn word-of-mouth into a brand that books sessions and tables. The problem

  • Beloved by neighbors but invisible to anyone beyond word-of-mouth radius
  • No name, no logo, no website — no way to book or be found
  • Every platform post a different size with a different look (or no posts at all)

The gap is rarely the cooking. It is the brand — and the brand is a system, not a logo file.

The direction

  • One identity: name, palette, voice — warm, confident, culturally specific
  • One content engine: a single source image exported at every platform's exact spec
  • One lead path: every post steers to the website → free guide → a first session
