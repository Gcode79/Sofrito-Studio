How the fixed price stays fixed

A fixed price protects you from surprise fees — and us from scope creep. Everything below is written into the estimate you sign, so "a small change" never silently becomes a second invoice.

Revision rounds

The Sprint includes two refinement rounds, priced up front. Feedback beyond that is billed as a follow-up — at the hourly add-on rate agreed in the estimate.

Feedback windows

We ask for feedback within 5 business days of a deliverable. If it slips past that without notice, the timeline slides rather than the price — and both of us keep a written record of every handoff.

What you provide

Final copy, recipes, photos, logos, or approvals come from you. Anything we have to research, write, or shoot beyond the written scope is quoted as an add-on before it starts — never surprise-billed after.

Payments

The Sprint splits into two payments — the Sofrito Session confirms your spot, and the balance settles before launch. Every invoice triggers on a written event, not a feeling.

Exact triggers per package
  • Sofrito Session: $400 paid up front to book; applies as a credit toward the Sprint booked within 30 days.
  • The Sprint: the remaining $597 settles before launch; the site goes live on your domain once the balance clears.

Final files release only after the final payment clears.

Extra scope, quoted early

An extra location, a second language menu, a new sub-brand, or a rush deadline — anything outside the signed scope gets a written quote and your sign-off before work begins.

Real life

If a Sprint delivery misses its window, you don't pay for that step. We communicate early and clearly on the plan — for the kitchen, not for margin. No surprise invoices either way.

What do I do if it's not working after we launch?

A brand isn't a weekend splash — it compounds. Here's the honest contract:

  • Included: two refinement rounds on the Sprint, plus 30 days of hands-on support.
  • If it's not working for you: tell us within those rounds and we fix it before it ships. If a delivery misses, you don't pay for that step.
  • What we won't promise: specific sales or placement numbers. Anyone who does is selling you a lottery ticket. We commit to craft, honesty, and delivery — the results we've shown are real and auditable.
