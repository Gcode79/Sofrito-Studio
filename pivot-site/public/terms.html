Terms

Simple terms for working together.

Sofrito Session and Brand & Web Sprint

The Sofrito Session is 45 minutes and costs $400. Start your sprint within 30 days of your session and the full $400 is credited toward the $997 Brand & Web Sprint. The remaining $597 is due after written direction lock. We launch within 48 hours — two business days — after the direction is set.

Direction lock is your written approval of the creative direction — the look, feel, and structure we agree on during or after your session. We confirm it by email within one business day of your session; your reply locks it.

Refunds

The $400 Sofrito Session is refundable in full if you request a refund within 7 days after your session and before direction lock. Paid but never had your session? You're covered too — request a full refund within 30 days of payment. Email support@sofritostudio.com to request one. Once the sprint starts, the session fee is non-refundable — it is credited in full toward your $997 sprint.
