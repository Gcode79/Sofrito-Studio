FAQ

Q: What if I hate it?

A: You approve the creative direction in writing before we build anything — so there are no surprises. Then you get two refinement rounds to dial it in. If the sprint isn't a fit for your business, we'll tell you on the session before any build work starts. We don't take on projects we can't stand behind.

Q: Is the $400 refundable?

A: The $400 Sofrito Session is non-refundable — it's a working strategy session, and the full fee is credited toward your $997 sprint when you start within 30 days. Need to move your session? One free reschedule with 48 hours' notice: email hello@sofritostudio.com. And if we decide on the session that the sprint isn't a fit for your business, we'll tell you straight — no further payment, no pressure.

Q: How fast is "48 hours"?

A: Your Sofrito Session sets the direction. We then build and launch the site within 48 hours — two business days from direction lock.

Q: What do I need to bring?

A: Your menu, your story, and any photos or branding you already have. We'll tell you exactly what's needed on the session.

Q: What does hosting cost?

A: We launch directly on your domain — that's included in the $997. If you want zero tech headaches after launch, our Engine Maintenance plan ($49/month) covers hosting, security, and small updates like menu changes. Prefer to handle it yourself? You get every file, ready to host anywhere.

Q: Do I own my website?

A: Yes. Once the sprint is paid in full, the brand assets, files, and site are yours outright — including if you ever leave the maintenance plan.

Q: What happens after the 30 days of support?

A: The 30 days after launch cover fixes and small tweaks. After that, the $49/month Engine Maintenance plan keeps you covered, or reach out anytime for project-based help.
