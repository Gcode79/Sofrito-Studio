## Content rules in force (from business-model.md)
- **Niche rule (strict):** all organic content is Sofrito Studio niche — food-business branding (identity, websites, naming, packaging, menus, launch, cultural-storytelling). No recipe-marketing; recipes exist only as top-of-funnel traffic steered to the studio.
- **Chain:** content → visit `sofritostudio.com` → lead (Digital Guide `/api/newsletter`, contact form, or session) → prospect → customer.
- **Research gate:** every post grounded in the research base; image spec at platform-exact size before commit (generators fail the gate otherwise).
- **Every CTA** = one of: take the Digital Guide, book a session, start a brand. Nothing else.
