What the $25 covers

One price, the whole job

  • The staging itself — a person places the furniture, scaled to the room, matched to its light.
  • The overlay check — the staged photo is blended against the original; walls, windows, floors, and fixtures must line up, or the photo does not ship.
  • The label — “Virtually Staged,” on the image, as your MLS expects it.
  • The disclosure line — ready to paste into your listing remarks.
  • The original, returned beside it — untouched, every time.
  • Speed — back within 24 hours from receipt of usable photos.

What the price never includes: a subscription, a minimum order, a setup fee, or a surprise at the end. The number on this page is the whole number.

The fair question

“Why not the $5 app?”

If you have the time to run the software and the eye to audit every image, the app is a fair choice — we will not pretend otherwise.

Here is the part the app leaves with you. When a staged photo quietly widens a window or erases a scuff, the app does not flag it. Your board’s disclosure rule does not care which tool made the picture — the listing agent owns it. Our $25 buys the two jobs the app hands back to you: a human check against the original, and the disclosure handled your MLS’s way.
