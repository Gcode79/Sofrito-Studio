Started at la cocina de abuela

Every project starts with the flavor truth. Sofrito is the base of the whole plate — the part you barely notice until it's missing. A brand should work the same way: the quiet foundation that makes everything taste better.

We never hand you a "brand recipe." We work with owners who are on the line every day, and we respect their clock: short documents, clear decisions, zero filler.

Why the name

Sofrito changes every house and every region, but it always does the same job: it makes the plate worth waiting for. That's the partnership we want — your food tells the story, we make the story taste as good as the food.

Why food first

A logo never made anyone hungry. Photos of the plate, an honest menu line, a story a regular can repeat — those do. So we design from the menu outward, not from the trend inward.

What we stand for

1. Taste first, brand second

We start with what actually makes your food different, then turn that into the identity. If the brand doesn't feed the craving, it's decoration.

2. Direct talk, no jargon

"Leverage" is banned in our studio. You run a kitchen, not a pitch deck. We write like people talk — warm, direct, and proud.

3. Fixed prices, honest scope

We quote the project, sign it, and keep the price. If your needs grow, we talk about it before anything extra is billed — never after.

4. Your business is a business

Brand is a growth lever, not art for its own sake. Every deliverable has to earn its place by pulling in customers, partners, or shelf space.
