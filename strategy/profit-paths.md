## Global assumptions and honest limits (apply to all scenarios)

- **Founder capacity is `UNKNOWN`.** Delivery load is stated per scenario as "units of work" without assuming one founder can carry it. If solo, scenarios B and C's project load implies freelancer/partner overflow (which is a cost decision).

## Scenario A — Retainer-led (recurring base)
**Shape: 8 × Essentials + 2 × Growth + 1 × Fractional**
| Stream | Qty | Mo revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | 8 | $12,000 |
| Growth | 2 | $5,000 |
| Fractional | 1 | $4,000 |
| **Monthly MRR** | | **$21,000** |

- Recurring, smoothest cash, best $/hour once content systems are templated.
- **Requires:** 11 active retainer accounts. Intake relies on the currently-inert waitlist (fix first), referral loop, and graduating project clients into retainers. Sustainable growth, long ramp: expect 6–12 months to fill, `UNKNOWN` fill-rate.
- **Risk:** concentration of delivery; cancels hurt MRR visibly. Solo founder with 11 content accounts is likely over-subscribed → workforce decision needed.
- **Decision it unlocks:** approve retainer capture + build the graduate-retention play (project → retainer handoff).

| The Sofrito | 6 | $15,000 |

| La Mesa | 3 | $22,500 |
| Projects subtotal | | $67,500 → **$22,500/mo avg** |
| Sessions | 4/mo | +$1,600/mo |

- One deal ≈ high value; La Mesa requires referral flow (referral policy is a decision).
- **Requires:** ~15 project bookings/quarter ≈ 5 concurrent-ish starts/quarter. Heavy delivery; float of ~4–5 plans in build at once typical for a small studio. Cash is lumpy — need 2–3 months of runway to absorb gaps (`UNKNOWN` runway).
- **Risk:** pipeline feast/famine; the D1 inflow evidence was QA artifacts (zero real demand proof), so ~15 qualified deals/quarter is not evidenced.
- **Decision it unlocks:** confirm La Mesa referral policy + referral incentive; decide freelance/partner capacity model.

## Scenario C — Hybrid (recommended baseline)
**Shape: 4 × Essentials + 1 × Growth retainer, plus 3 Plato + 2 Sofrito + 1 La Mesa per quarter, plus 6 sessions/mo**
| Stream | Amount |
|---|---|
| Essentials 4 | $6,000/mo |
| Growth 1 | $2,500/mo |
| Projects (rolling quarter $27,500) | ~$9,170/mo avg |
| Sessions 6 | $2,400/mo |
| **Monthly average** | **~$20,070** |

- **Why recommended:** retainers give a base that absorbs project gaps; projects give the big beats and case-study wins; sessions are the low-friction door (and feed projects). Any single stream failing is survivable for the month.
- **Requires:** ~7 deals/quarter (self-fundable pace), 5 retainer accounts (deliverable by one person with systems or a junior), steady 6 sessions/mo (≈ 1.5/wk).
- **Risk:** the broadest surface = needs all three funnels kept alive; attention split across products.
- **Decision it unlocks:** approve triage + the "sessions→projects→retainers" graduation play as the core motion.

## Shared ramp logic (all scenarios)
1. **Month 0–1:** verify real capture (D1 pool was QA-only), seed email templates, enable retainer capture, stopgap session copy, qualify enrichment prospects. Goal: first real conversations, first session bookings, first wins recorded in D1.
2. **Month 2–4:** sessions + 1st project clients graduate; first retainer signed; case studies captured. Goal: first $3–6k month from real deals.
3. **Month 5–12:** scale whichever stream over-performs (A/B/C pivots inside the same price deck); add capacity only against provable demand.

## Founder decisions required to make ANY scenario runnable
- Retainer waitlist capture (O1) — go/no-go.
- Booking/second checkout fix (O2) — choose A (deploy) or B (stopgap) now.
- Brand Foundation (O3) — price/fold/drop.
- La Mesa policy (O5) — scarcity vs volume.
- Tier 3 (O6) — approve/defer/kill.
- Approval to pursue a real-demand source: qualify the social-scan enrichment prospects and/or run capture validation (every scenario needs real inflow data to start).
