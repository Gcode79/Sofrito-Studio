Pricing & scope

How do fixed prices stay fixed?

The estimate is signed before we start and it doesn't drift. Every project lists its revision rounds up front (1–2 per deliverable), feedback happens inside a 5-business-day window, and anything outside the signed scope gets a written quote and your sign-off before work begins. No surprise bills, on either side.

What's included in the Sprint?

The Sprint ($997) is delivered in 48 hours: a Core Brand Identity (logo suite, palette, typography, brand voice) and a 1-Page Engine — a fast site with your menu, ordering links, hours and location, zero hosting fees. Includes 30 days of support after delivery.

What is NOT included in a project?

Photo shoots, paid ad spend and ad management, and ongoing content work aren't part of the one-time Sprint. Anything you need beyond the written scope is quoted as an add-on before it starts — an extra location, a second-language menu, a new sub-brand, or a rush deadline.

Do you offer ongoing content or retainer work?

Sofrito Studio keeps it to two offers right now: the Sofrito Session and the Brand & Web Sprint. Ongoing content retainers are on the waitlist; when a spot opens they're month-to-month and cancelable with 30 days' notice.

Process & timing

How long does a project take?

The Sprint ships in 48 hours, start to finish. The Sofrito Session is 90 minutes — a strategy call, not a project. Timelines slide if feedback slips past the window, but the price never slides.

How do payments and milestones work?

The Sofrito Session is $400, paid up front to book, and applies as a credit toward the Sprint booked within 30 days. The Sprint's remaining $597 settles before launch. Every invoice triggers on a written event — never a feeling. Final files release only after the balance clears.

Why only two concepts instead of more options?

Because more options is how good work drowns. Two researched directions is the professional standard — each one shown with the thinking behind it, so you understand the "why" before you react. Fewer, sharper choices mean the right direction gets chosen instead of the safest one. If the direction isn't right, we refine it specifically — never by random guessing.

How do revisions actually work?

Every deliverable includes 1–2 revision rounds, and your feedback lands in one consolidated response inside a 5-business-day window. Specific feedback — "the color feels too dark, I want it warmer" — goes straight to the board; vague feedback like "I don't love it" gets one quick clarifying question first. A brand-new direction after both rounds is quoted as an add-on (from $250), so the scope and the quality both stay protected.

Do you work with existing businesses or only new ones?

Both. A rebrand or reset is a classic Sprint use case, and existing restaurants, trucks, salsa and sauce makers, and packaged food brands all fit. If you're not sure you fit, a Sofrito Session gives you a clear yes or no — you keep the plan either way.

Can I try a session before committing to a project?

Yes. The Sofrito Session is a 90-minute one-on-one for $400: we read your brand, answer your questions, and hand you a written action plan you keep even if you never hire us. Money-back if it isn't worth it, no questions — and your $400 applies as a credit toward the Sprint booked within 30 days.

Who it's for

Who should hire Sofrito Studio?

Independent food businesses — restaurants, food trucks and pop-ups, salsa and hot sauce makers, pantry brands, and specialty packaged food. You're a fit if you'd rather pay for honest direction than a glossy brand book, you have a launch or rebrand this year, and you can make a decision in a couple of weeks.

Who should NOT hire Sofrito Studio?

Anyone who just wants a cheap logo, who expects a brand alone to make sales, who wants AI-run content with no human review, or whose business isn't food. We'll tell you that up front instead of taking your money.

Proof & guarantees

How can we trust you without a wall of testimonials?

We don't post invented quotes or fake growth charts. The proof is the studio's own live rebrand — this website is a Sofrito Studio deliverable — plus concept work clearly labeled as concepts. Real client results will appear only with their written permission. No glossy marketing fluff. We build the brand foundation that takes your food from a local secret to a packed house.

What happens if a project isn't working after launch?

Every Sprint includes refinement rounds and 30 days of hands-on support. If a delivery misses, you don't pay for that step. What we won't promise is specific sales or placement numbers — we commit to craft, honesty, and delivery.
