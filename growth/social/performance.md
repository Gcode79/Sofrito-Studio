## Monthly review checklist (run at end of each month)

- [ ] Qualified demand this month vs previous (bookings/inquiries/real D1 rows)
- [ ] Which 3 posts produced the most qualified signal; keep their angles
- [ ] Which posts produced vanity only; archive or rework
- [ ] Response-log review: recurring questions → new concept or response rail
- [ ] Platform verdict refresh (keep / deprioritize) against actual evidence
- [ ] Update strategy + next month's calendar; record findings in `ops/now.md`

## Attribution honesty rule

- No post is credited with bookings unless the session/inquiry can actually be traced to it (mention of the post, UTM, or referenced content).
- If tracking isn't reliably in place (e.g., stopgap session path), we say UNABLE TO MEASURE rather than assume credit.
