## Response rails

### Rail R1 — Genuine praise / compliment
> Thank them warmly, name the specific thing, redirect to usefulness (save the post, or "want the same for your menu?") without selling hard.

### Rail R2 — "How much does X cost?"
> Give the real number from the fact library when it's public; for pre-rollout offers (retainer/La Mesa) say it's pending founder finalization, and offer the Session as the decision-friendly start.

### Rail R3 — "Do you have clients like me?"
> Honest: no paid client work exists yet; current proof is our own rebrand + spec concepts, openly labeled. Emphasize the Session as a low-risk way to see how the approach fits their business. Never imply paid social proof we don't have.

### Rail R4 — "Can you just do X task?" (scope question)
> Confirm scope against the ladder; small/quick items → point to the Session; project-size → point to Sofrito/Plato scope pages. If it's genuinely outside scope, say so and be helpful with a resource, not a hook.

### Rail R5 — Price-shopping / "too expensive"
> Commiserate, break down what the money is for (scope transparency), don't defend the price, and don't apologize. Offer the Session as the review-scale option. If it's not a fit, say so kindly.

### Rail R6 — Community / "I'm just starting" (low-obligation)
> Warm, generous, one concrete next action (one thing: menu > pricing > photos > site). No hard CTA unless they ask.
