## Sequence (same 3-step for all; swap the personalized hook per lead)

### Email 1 — Day 0 · Subject: `[hook]` (short, no "just wanted to reach out")
Hook examples:
- Growee: `real food people should be seen eating this` — nah. Better: `you two + a bigger audience`
- El Chilar: `500 SKUs, 16 states, no face behind it`
- Paldy: `you're scaling faster than your creative can keep up`
- Empanada Lady: `your food sells out. your brand shouldn't sell itself short`
Actually: keep it simple & human. Pattern: `quick one about [their thing]`

Body shape (short — 5 sentences max):
```
Hi [Name],

[1 line: what I saw / what I noticed about them — specific, proves I looked].

I run Sofrito Studio — a brand studio for food businesses. We do what a shy-but-talented kitchen needs: brand identity, websites, and a content system so the right people actually hear about you.

[1 line: how that maps to THEIR specific gap].

We keep a small waitlist for food brands we'd love to work with, since we only take on a few new clients each month. Happy to add you to it — no pressure at all — or we can just have the 15-min chat and see if it's a fit.

— [Your name]
Sofrito Studio · [sig]
```

### Waitlist framing (the "we're in demand" lever — kept warm, never pushy)
- Core claim to stand behind: **"we take on a limited number of new clients per month; a waitlist keeps quality high."** If you're not capping capacity yet, either (a) start capping at e.g. 4 new clients/mo, or (b) frame it as a *content-retainer waitlist* (Essentials/Growth fills fast) rather than a lie about booked-out volume. Pick one and stay consistent in replies.
- Delivery rules: always soft ("no pressure", "if you'd ever like to", "I'd love to"), never scarcity-shame ("before it closes"), never implies they're wasting time. Invitation, not pressure.
- If they ask "what's the waitlist for?" answer plainly: monthly spots for retainer clients (Content/Essentials/Growth), first-come. Real, not manufactured urgency.

## Rules
- One email per lead per step; 3 steps max; stop on ANY reply (always switch to human mode).
- Always include: real name, what Sofrito Studio is, a specific observation about THEIR brand, one clear ask.
- No attachments in email 1-2; samples sent only after they say yes (email 2 asks permission first).
- Track replies in Google Sheets (`Lead` rows) — add `outreach` column.
- CAN-SPAM: every email has real identity + no deception; add a one-line "not interested → just reply and I'll stop".
