# First-reply draft (S3) — never send automatically

Use as a base. Fill the bracketed parts, keep it under 90 words, warm and direct.
PR register. No buzzwords (leverage, synergy, innovative, cutting-edge,
passionate, game-changer, delicious, authentic). No emojis.

```
TITLE: Re: <business_name>

Oye <name>,

Gracias por contar de <business_name>. De lo que escribiste, lo que me
(clicó más / dio más curiosidad) fue [phrase from their message]. So 
[1-line specific reaction: remind them of the 8-second angle, or 
summarize the opportunity in their own words].

Since you mentioned [budget/package_interest], let's talk for 20 minutes
this week — [90-min free session, /session]. Pick a time here: [calendar link].

— <your name> at Sofrito Studio
```

Notes:
- Replying inside the 24h window is the single biggest conversion lever.
- If the lead has score > 70 or described a concrete project, book the session.
- If they asked a question, answer the question first, then talk.
