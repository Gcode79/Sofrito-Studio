The Operating System

Inside the 48 Hours

The clock starts when direction is set — confirmed in writing — not when you pay. Here is exactly what happens on each side of that line.

A

Before the clock starts

  • Kickoff questionnaire — one form: your menu, your story, your photos.
  • Direction call, 30 minutes — we lock the visual lane and the one action your page must drive.
  • Written confirmation + $597 balance — direction is not set until you confirm it in writing and the balance clears.
B

The 48-hour build

  • Hours 0–16 — identity. Logo set, color palette, typography, and the rationale behind them.
  • Hours 16–36 — the page. Built mobile-first with your real content. One page, one action.
  • Hours 36–44 — V1 walkthrough. A video tour of every choice and why we made it.
  • Hours 44–48 — refinement + launch. Your consolidated feedback, then we deploy to your domain.
C

Fixed scope

  • In: one brand identity and one one-page website. That is the sprint.
  • Out: monthly hosting is separate. Anything beyond the one page and identity is a different engagement — never scope creep inside your 48 hours.
  • Feedback rule: one consolidated response per round. Three people, one list — that is how 48 hours stays 48 hours.

FAQ

Q: Is the $400 refundable?

A: Yes — in full, if you request it within 7 days after your session and before direction lock. Paid but never had the session? Full refund within 30 days of payment. Email support@sofritostudio.com. Once the sprint starts, the fee is non-refundable and credited toward your $997 sprint.

Q: How fast is "48 hours"?

A: Your Sofrito Session sets the direction. We then build and launch the site within 48 hours — two business days from direction lock.

Q: What do I need to bring?

A: Your menu, your story, and any photos or branding you already have. We'll tell you exactly what's needed on the session.

Q: What happens after I pay?

A: Pick your Sofrito Session time right after payment. We set the direction on the call — then you approve it in writing (that's direction lock; we confirm it by email within one business day), and we build and launch within 48 hours.

Q: What does hosting cost?

A: Hosting is separate from the $997 sprint. Our Engine Maintenance plan is $49/month and covers hosting, security, and small updates. It's optional — your site is yours either way.

Q: Do I own my website?

A: Yes. Once the sprint is paid in full, the brand assets, files, and site are yours outright — including if you ever leave the maintenance plan.

Q: What happens after the 30 days of support?

A: The 30 days after launch cover fixes and small tweaks. After that, the $49/month Engine Maintenance plan keeps you covered, or reach out anytime for project-based help.
