The line item nobody prints: your time

The DIY route looks free because the invoice is small. The real bill is your hours — setup, menu changes, fixes, and every “I’ll get to it this weekend” that turns into a month of an unfinished site. We can’t price your time for you. We can tell you ours costs $0 of it: you approve a direction, we do the work.

Questions owners ask about cost

How much does a food truck website cost in 2026?

It depends on the route. A DIY builder runs $0 to $50 a month plus your own weekends. A traditional agency typically charges $5,000 to $15,000 or more, billed by the hour, over 6 to 12 weeks. A POS-bundled site is priced inside your POS contract, and you lose it if you leave the POS. The Sofrito Studio sprint is $997 flat — brand identity and a one-page website, live within two business days of written direction lock, owned outright.

Is a DIY website builder actually cheaper?

In dollars, yes — $0 to $50 a month. In time, no: setup, menu updates, fixes, and maintenance all fall on you, and completion time varies. DIY also rents you the platform: stop paying and the site goes dark. The honest comparison is $0 to $600 in year one plus your weekends, against $997 once for a finished site you own.
